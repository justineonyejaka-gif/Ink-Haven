# Ink Haven

A home for every idea. A phone-first writing app you can install on your phone.

## Put it online (GitHub Pages)
1. Upload everything in this folder to your repository, keeping the same folders (the `icons` folder must stay).
2. Settings, then Pages, then Deploy from a branch: main, / (root), Save.
3. Open your link, then use the browser menu and choose **Install app** or **Add to Home screen**.

## Good to know
- After the first visit the app keeps working offline. It opens from your phone even if the website is blocked on your network.
- When you upload a new `index.html`, also change `VERSION` in `sw.js` (for example inkhaven-v2) so phones pick up the update.
- Your writing is saved inside the browser on the device you use. Export what matters.
- AI help needs a small server with a key, so it only works inside Claude for now.
